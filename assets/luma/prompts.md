```text

EXACTLY 16 separate sprites laid out in a regular 4 columns by 4 rows grid, each cell 256x256. No visible grid, text, labels or borders. Each cell contains the SAME small original adult adventurer, same outfit and proportions: short silver-white hair, teal blue-green tunic and short cape, burnt-orange scarf, brown boots, dark belt, little round bronze shield, short silver sword. Expressive elegant late-16-bit / modern pixel-art game character, clean readable silhouette, dark colored outlines, detailed but not noisy, warm highlights. Camera is consistent orthographic overhead three-quarter view as in a classic top-down adventure game, NOT side-view platformer, NOT isometric, no perspective floor. Row 2 faces EAST/to viewer's right. Row 3 faces NORTH/away from viewer with back visible. Row 4 faces WEST/to viewer's left. In EACH row the four columns are: (1) neutral standing sword lowered; (2) walk stride left foot forward with subtle opposite arm swing; (3) walk stride right foot forward; (4) energetic sword slash attack pose with silver blade extended in the facing direction, small blue-white slash arc within the cell. The center body and head scale must remain consistent across all 16 cells. Preserve all four rows and four columns, no extra sprites, no objects in unused cell space. This sheet will be sliced into 16 game animation frames and displayed at 64x64 pixels per cell, so prioritize a beautiful crisp readable character silhouette. Absolutely transparent empty space around each sprite; no colored background, no checkerboard painted into the image, no mock game scene, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```
